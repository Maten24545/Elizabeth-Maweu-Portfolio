About Me

Learning & Development Professional

I am a Learning & Development professional focused on turning learning into measurable workplace performance. I design and facilitate practical, engaging learning experiences that help employees build the knowledge, skills, confidence, and behaviours required to perform effectively in their roles.

My experience spans onboarding, induction, capability development, customer service, sales, marketing, call-centre operations, product knowledge, compliance, and communication—supporting both customer-facing teams and back-office functions. I approach learning with a strong focus on business needs, learner experience, practical application, coaching, and performance assessment.

I believe effective L&D goes beyond delivering training. It is about understanding performance gaps, designing relevant learning interventions, creating opportunities for practice, and equipping employees to apply what they learn with confidence.

I am particularly passionate about creating learning environments where employees feel supported to ask questions, practise new skills, receive constructive feedback, and continuously improve—ultimately contributing to stronger individual performance, more capable teams, and better business outcomes.

Featured Work

Signature L&D Project

Two-Week Employee Onboarding Programme

The Challenge

New employees need more than product knowledge to become productive. They need to understand the organisation, systems, customers, processes, compliance requirements, and behavioural expectations.

My Approach

I structured onboarding into a progressive learning journey that combines:

Company Induction Product Knowledge Systems Training Customer Experience Sales Communication Compliance Objection Handling Needs Analysis Role Plays Practical Exercises Coaching Observation Knowledge Assessments Final Performance Assessment

Learning Methodology

The programme uses a blended, learner-centred approach combining modules, facilitated learning, role plays, discussions, coaching, and assessment to improve practical application.

Learning Outcomes

By the end of the programme, learners are expected to demonstrate:

  • Product and process knowledge
  • Understanding of compliance requirements
  • Effective customer communication
  • Ability to navigate relevant systems
  • Confidence handling customer interactions
  • Application of techniques for sales objection
  • Readiness to transition into the workplace

Business Focus: The programme is designed to shorten the gap between being trained and being productive, while creating a consistent onboarding experience for new employees.
