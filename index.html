SIGNATURE L&D PROJECT

TWO-WEEK EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING PROGRAMME

The Challenge

New employees need more than product knowledge to become productive. They need to understand the organisation, systems, customers, processes, compliance requirements and behavioural expectations.

My Approach

I structured onboarding into a progressive learning journey that combines:

  • Company induction
  • Product knowledge
  • Systems training
  • Customer experience
  • Sales communication
  • Compliance
  • Objection handling
  • Needs analysis
  • Role plays
  • Practical exercises
  • Coaching
  • Observation
  • Knowledge assessments
  • Final performance assessment

Learning Methodology

The programme uses a blended, learner-centred approach combining modules, facilitated learning, discussions, role plays to improve practical application, coaching and assessment.

Learning Outcome

By the end of the programme, learners are expected to demonstrate:

  • Product and process knowledge
  • Understanding of compliance requirements
  • Effective customer communication
  • Ability to navigate relevant systems
  • Confidence handling customer interactions
  • Application of techniques for sales objection
  • Readiness to transition into the workplace

Business Focus

The programme is designed to shorten the gap between being trained and being productive, while creating a consistent onboarding experience for new employees.
